# Clinical onboarding and doctor-selected hospitals/clinics

October18, 2026, Asia/Tehran. User approved the exact pages and controls in `clinical-onboarding-locations-proposal-2026-10-18.md`, including optional admin/root verification when creating a doctor. Existing dirty/untracked work and historical evidence are retained.

## Before and after

Before: the hospital directory model existed without panel creation/detail controls. Clinical directories had no creation pages. The legacy doctor-account creation path did not create a doctor profile. Doctors could schedule only preassigned locations, and fallback appointment generation used catalog pricing. The Workplace label obscured the distinction between an organization and its association with a doctor.

After: admin/root get role-fixed `/panel/doctors/new` and `/panel/patients/new`, entered from their existing directories. They get `/panel/hospitals`, `/panel/hospitals/new` and `/panel/hospitals/:hospitalId` (public ULID), with searchable/paged organizational cards, box/grid details and guarded edits. The grouped People navigation contains Hospitals/Clinics. Existing Staff `/panel/users` remains root-only; no new role is introduced. `/panel/schedules/workplaces` redirects to `/panel/schedules`; real assignment detail keeps `/panel/schedules/workplaces/:workplaceId`.

Workplace labels become Hospitals/Clinics in English and corresponding Farsi terms. Advanced numeric filters identify **doctor–hospital/clinic assignment IDs**, preserving the technical distinction from an organization's public ULID. No internal field/route/database identifiers are renamed.

Doctor/client creation accepts identity/contact fields and a strong confirmed initial password only as input. Responses/support reads/audits exclude those private values and credentials. Doctor creation also creates a real profile, primary specialty association, experience and base fee. Verification defaults pending; an explicit creating admin/root checkbox may set verified/verified_at/verified_by under the same guarded transaction and audit. Doctor self-service cannot grant verification. Legacy doctor creation also gets an unverified minimal profile; its existing credential and staff-role controls remain intact.

On their existing profile, doctors use Add hospital/clinic to search active unassigned organizations, choose offered active services and enter their own prices/currencies. Each association gets independent full-day/shift/rest schedules through the accepted working-hours editor. Adding a location publishes no slots. Existing cards show organizational city/address and the doctor's service-price defaults. Manage my services & prices updates defaults/adds offerings without deleting existing service history or rewriting published slots. Closing future working days remains the ordinary guarded weekly-save operation; organization deletion, affiliation removal/reactivation and post-creation verification workflows remain separate.

## Pricing and safeguards

Organizations have no price column or price controls. The existing association pivot is the doctor's service price at that hospital/clinic. Newly materialized appointments use a doctor shift override, otherwise the doctor's service default, otherwise the doctor's profile fee. Booking cards and admin schedule detail use that same doctor base fallback. Currency uses existing configured/service/override handling; no currency conversion or hospital commission is introduced. Existing materialized slots, reservations, prices, payment summaries and history remain untouched. Existing guarded slot overrides remain available; default-price edits take effect with newly published hours, not by rewriting old slots.

All new writes require fresh active role/ownership under locks, allowlisted fields, reason, explicit confirmation, recent password and atomic audit. Hospital edits and doctor changes require fresh HMAC revisions; selected hospital details are separately revision-bound. Duplicates, inactive/archived organizations/services, foreign doctor IDs, stale revisions and price no-ops refuse. New audit actions explicitly allowlist organizational state, doctor onboarding metadata and doctor-service price before/after values; they do not record credentials or private identity inputs. Source uses existing tables, roles, policies and runtime; no dependency/schema/env/credential replacement. Native forms do not automatically resubmit after uncertain delivery. Read hooks bind data to the current actor/path and abort on unmount; write hooks abort on session invalidation and retain only a pending flag, never a credential/form body, in session storage.
