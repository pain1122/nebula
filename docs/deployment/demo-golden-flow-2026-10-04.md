## Presentation Sequence

1. Prepare one verified doctor, active workplace/checkup offering, server-owned price and available schedule, patient account and fixed questionnaire.
2. Patient selects the checkup, doctor/workplace and available slot, then books. Show the real reservation, pending hold and server-owned price.
3. Patient completes deterministic sandbox checkout. Verified, idempotent payment processing confirms the reservation; no manual paid-state shortcut.
4. Assigned doctor requests a test/result for the reservation.
5. Patient uploads a private file to their own open request. Quarantine, content/integrity validation and scanning must approve the file before doctor access. October 8 content source/isolated parser acceptance is recorded in [the validation report](medical-content-validation-2026-10-08.md); live scanner/worker/parser activation and UI remain open.
6. Assigned doctor reviews the eligible result and publishes the patient-visible outcome. Appointment completion must also satisfy the existing started/confirmed/paid requirements.
7. Patient views the authorized published outcome; drafts, internal notes and unsafe files remain inaccessible.
8. Patient submits the fixed questionnaire and sees its score/recommendation as a separate branch, not an invented booking prerequisite.

The flow uses real APIs and persisted records. Preconfigured setup narrows presentation breadth; catalog CRUD and broader administration stay in the full backlog.
